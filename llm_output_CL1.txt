### Test Case: Verify Modernized Audit Additions - DIS > Generate Disclosures Fields

---

#### Test Case ID:  
`MOD_AUDIT_DIS_001`

#### User Story:  
Modernized Audit additions - DIS > Generate Disclosures Fields

#### Description:  
This test case validates the addition of new fields to the Modernized Audit functionality in the DIS > Generate Disclosures section. The fields include HPML, Intent to Proceed, Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement, and Mortgage Broker License Type. The test ensures proper rendering, dropdown options, privilege restrictions, and functionality of these fields.

#### Precondition:  
1. A loan is created in the Broker Portal with the following attributes:  
   - Channel: CL1  
   - Loan Purpose: Refinance  
   - Loan Type: Conventional  
   - Product: CF30  
   - Loan Stage: Created  
2. User has appropriate access to the DIS > Generate Disclosures section.

---

### Test Steps and Expected Results:

#### **HPML Field Validation**

1. **Step No:** 1  
   **Description:** Log in to the H2O-A application in the UAT environment.  
   **Expected Result:** Login should be successful.

2. **Step No:** 2  
   **Description:** Navigate to `DIS > Generate Disclosures > Generate Disclosure`.  
   **Expected Result:** The "Generate Disclosure" screen should be displayed.

3. **Step No:** 3  
   **Description:** Verify that the "HPML" field is rendered as a Dropdown.  
   **Expected Result:** The "HPML" field should be displayed as a dropdown.

4. **Step No:** 4  
   **Description:** Verify that the "HPML" dropdown contains exactly the following options:  
   - Yes  
   - No  
   **Expected Result:** The dropdown should display only the options "Yes" and "No".

5. **Step No:** 5  
   **Description:** Verify that the "HPML" field is not privilege restricted.  
   **Expected Result:** The field should be accessible to all users without privilege restrictions.

---

#### **Intent to Proceed Field Validation**

6. **Step No:** 6  
   **Description:** Navigate to `DIS > Generate Disclosures`.  
   **Expected Result:** The "Generate Disclosures" screen should be displayed.

7. **Step No:** 7  
   **Description:** Verify that the "Intent to Proceed" field is rendered as a Checkbox.  
   **Expected Result:** The "Intent to Proceed" field should be displayed as a checkbox.

8. **Step No:** 8  
   **Description:** Verify that the "Intent to Proceed" checkbox can be checked and unchecked.  
   **Expected Result:** The checkbox should allow toggling between checked and unchecked states.

9. **Step No:** 9  
   **Description:** Verify that the "Intent to Proceed" field is not privilege restricted.  
   **Expected Result:** The field should be accessible to all users without privilege restrictions.

---

#### **Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement Field Validation**

10. **Step No:** 10  
    **Description:** Navigate to `DIS > Generate Disclosures > Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement`.  
    **Expected Result:** The "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" screen should be displayed.

11. **Step No:** 11  
    **Description:** Verify that the "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" field is rendered as a Dropdown.  
    **Expected Result:** The "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" field should be displayed as a dropdown.

12. **Step No:** 12  
    **Description:** Verify that the "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" dropdown contains exactly the following options:  
    - Yes  
    - No  
    **Expected Result:** The dropdown should display only the options "Yes" and "No".

13. **Step No:** 13  
    **Description:** Verify that the "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" field is restricted based on user privilege.  
    **Expected Result:** The field should only be accessible to users with the appropriate privileges.

---

#### **Mortgage Broker License Type Field Validation**

14. **Step No:** 14  
    **Description:** Navigate to `DIS > Generate Disclosures > Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement`.  
    **Expected Result:** The "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" screen should be displayed.

15. **Step No:** 15  
    **Description:** Verify that the "Mortgage Broker License Type" field is rendered as a Dropdown.  
    **Expected Result:** The "Mortgage Broker License Type" field should be displayed as a dropdown.

16. **Step No:** 16  
    **Description:** Verify that the "Mortgage Broker License Type" dropdown contains exactly the following options:  
    - CFL  
    - DRE  
    - RML  
    **Expected Result:** The dropdown should display only the options "CFL", "DRE", and "RML".

17. **Step No:** 17  
    **Description:** Verify that the "Mortgage Broker License Type" field is restricted based on user privilege.  
    **Expected Result:** The field should only be accessible to users with the appropriate privileges.

18. **Step No:** 18  
    **Description:** Verify that the "Mortgage Broker License Type" field appears only when `SubPropState = CA`.  
    **Expected Result:** The field should be displayed only when the SubPropState is set to "CA".

---

### Audit Trail Validation

19. **Step No:** 19  
    **Description:** Navigate to the `Audit Trail` tab and verify that an entry is created for any changes made to the fields.  
    **Expected Result:** The audit trail should capture the following details for each field change:  
    - User who made the change  
    - Time Occurred  
    - Previous Value  
    - New Value  
    - Field Name  

---

### Post-Condition:  
1. Ensure all changes made during the test are reverted to their original state.  
2. Log out of the H2O-A application.

---

### Notes:  
- Privilege restrictions for "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" and "Mortgage Broker License Type" fields should be validated with both privileged and non-privileged users.  
- Ensure that the dropdown options are case-sensitive and match exactly as specified.  
- Verify that the audit trail entries are accurate and complete for all field changes.  

--- 

### End of Test Case
