### Test Case: Modernized Audit Additions - DIS > Generate Disclosures Fields

---

#### Test Case ID:  
`MOD_AUDIT_DIS_001`

#### Test Case Title:  
Validation of Modernized Audit Additions for DIS > Generate Disclosures Fields

#### User Story:  
Modernized Audit additions - DIS > Generate Disclosures Fields

#### Description:  
This test case validates the addition of new fields to the Modernized Audit functionality in the DIS > Generate Disclosures section. The fields include HPML, Intent to Proceed, Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement, and Mortgage Broker License Type. The test ensures that these fields are rendered correctly, contain the expected options, and adhere to privilege restrictions where applicable.

---

### Preconditions:
1. A loan must be created in the Customer Portal with the following attributes:
   - **Channel**: RTL
   - **Loan Purpose**: Purchase
   - **Loan Type**: Conventional
   - **Product**: CF30
   - **Loan Stage**: Application Accepted
2. DIS > LE Fees > Rate Information:
   - The radio button "Borrower will receive a __ % / $ $0.00 __ Lender Credit for the interest rate of __ %" should be selected.
3. User must have appropriate privileges to access the Modernized Audit and DIS > Generate Disclosures sections.

---

### Acceptance Criteria:
1. **HPML Field**:
   - Verify that the "HPML" field is rendered as a dropdown.
   - Verify that the dropdown contains the options: "Yes" and "No".
   - Verify that the field is not privilege restricted.

2. **Intent to Proceed Field**:
   - Verify that the "Intent to Proceed" field is rendered as a checkbox.
   - Verify that the checkbox can be checked and unchecked.
   - Verify that the field is not privilege restricted.

3. **Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement Field**:
   - Verify that the "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" field is rendered as a dropdown.
   - Verify that the dropdown contains the options: "Yes" and "No".
   - Verify that the field is restricted based on user privilege.

4. **Mortgage Broker License Type Field**:
   - Verify that the "Mortgage Broker License Type" field is rendered as a dropdown.
   - Verify that the dropdown contains the options: "CFL", "DRE", and "RML".
   - Verify that the field is restricted based on user privilege.
   - Verify that the field appears only when `SubPropState = CA`.

---

### Test Steps:

#### Step 1: Log in to the System
- **Description**: Log in to the H2O-A system in the UAT1 environment.
- **Expected Result**: Login should be successful.

- **Description**: Verify the "HPML" field.

  - Verify that the dropdown contains the options: "Yes" and "No".

- **Expected Result**: The "HPML" field should meet all the criteria.

#### Step 5: Navigate to DIS > Generate Disclosures
- **Description**: Navigate to the "Generate Disclosures" section.
- **Expected Result**: The "Generate Disclosures" screen should be displayed.

#### Step 6: Validate the Intent to Proceed Field
- **Description**: Verify the "Intent to Proceed" field.
  - Check that the field is rendered as a checkbox.
  - Verify that the checkbox can be checked and unchecked.
  - Confirm that the field is not privilege restricted.
- **Expected Result**: The "Intent to Proceed" field should meet all the criteria.

#### Step 7: Navigate to DIS > Generate Disclosures > Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement
- **Description**: Navigate to the "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" section under DIS > Generate Disclosures.
- **Expected Result**: The "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" screen should be displayed.

#### Step 8: Validate the Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement Field
- **Description**: Verify the "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" field.
  - Check that the field is rendered as a dropdown.
  - Verify that the dropdown contains the options: "Yes" and "No".
  - Confirm that the field is restricted based on user privilege.
- **Expected Result**: The "Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement" field should meet all the criteria.

#### Step 9: Validate the Mortgage Broker License Type Field
- **Description**: Verify the "Mortgage Broker License Type" field.
  - Check that the field is rendered as a dropdown.
  - Verify that the dropdown contains the options: "CFL", "DRE", and "RML".
  - Confirm that the field is restricted based on user privilege.
  - Verify that the field appears only when `SubPropState = CA`.
- **Expected Result**: The "Mortgage Broker License Type" field should meet all the criteria.

#### Step 10: Navigate to Tools > Audit
- **Description**: Navigate to the "Audit" section under Tools.
- **Expected Result**: The Audit popup should be displayed.

#### Step 11: Search for Fields in Audit
- **Description**: Search for the following fields in the Audit section:
  - HPML
  - Intent to Proceed
  - Mortgage Broker Fee/Compensation Agreement
  - Mortgage Broker License Type
- **Expected Result**: Each field should be present in the Audit section with the following details:

  - Previous Value

#### Step 12: Logout
- **Description**: Log out of the H2O-A system.
- **Expected Result**: Logout should be successful.
